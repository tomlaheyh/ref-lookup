The Ref button

Every DOI on a page gets a Ref button. Red: the article is retracted, or is a retraction notice. Red outline: a key author has other retracted papers. Hover: times cited, free full text, preprint, the journal's SJR rank (SCImago, bundled) and its field-weighted citation impact (FWCI), from OpenAlex and CrossRef. On PubMed the buttons pause while PubMed Citation Bar is running.

Styles

APA 7th, MLA 9th, Chicago 18th (bibliography entry), AMA 11th, Vancouver/ICMJE, and RIS for reference managers. Data comes from the DOI's record at doi.org, so a citation is only as complete as the publisher's record.

Where we differ from Zotero, on purpose

  • Article numbers. Many journals number articles instead of paging them (eBioMedicine 105834, PLOS e0120513). APA 7 writes "118, Article 105834"; MLA 9 leaves the number out; Chicago, AMA and Vancouver give the number where the pages would go. Zotero stores the number as a page, so its APA has no "Article" and its MLA shows "p. 105834".
  • Vancouver. Six authors, then "et al." when there are more than six (NLM), and the DOI as "doi:10.…". Zotero can switch to its web-page form ("[Internet]", "[cited …]", "Available from:") when the item carries a URL.

Zotero's single 'quotes' come from its UK English setting; MLA and Chicago use double quotes.

Known limits

  • Title case. APA, AMA and Vancouver want sentence case. A title the publisher sent in Title Case is converted: only common English words (bundled words.csv) are lowered, so names, places and acronyms keep their capitals. A note above the citations names any capitals kept, to check by hand.
  • Editors and place. CrossRef rarely gives editors or a publisher's city for book chapters, so those parts are often missing. The site does not read editors yet; the extension does when they exist.
  • Dates. The year is the earliest publication date. MLA and Chicago add the issue's month when it falls in the same year.
  • Pages box. Typing pages in the Cite panel replaces what the record gave; leaving it as filled in keeps article numbers working.
